About ToolNest

ToolNest is a small collection of practical browser tools for everyday writing and calculations. The aim is to keep common tasks simple and easy to use.

Privacy

The tools in this version process your text and calculations in your browser. This version does not have accounts or send your tool inputs to a ToolNest server. If we add advertising, analytics or other third-party services, we will update this notice to explain their data practices.
