How to use this word counter

  1. Type your text or paste it into the text box.
  2. See your word and character counts update automatically.
  3. Copy your text or clear the box when you finish.

What does the tool count?

Words: Groups of text separated by whitespace.

Characters: All characters, including spaces and line breaks.

Characters without spaces: Characters remaining after whitespace is removed.

Sentences: Sentence-ending punctuation followed by whitespace or the end of the text.

Paragraphs: Non-empty text blocks separated by blank lines.

Is my text private?

This page calculates your counts directly in your browser. Its word-counting code does not send the text to a ToolNest server. Other browser extensions or services may have their own data practices.

Who can use a word counter?

Students can check assignments, writers can monitor article length, and professionals can review reports, emails and other documents. Always follow any specific word-count rules supplied by your school, publisher or client.
