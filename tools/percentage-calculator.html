How to calculate a percentage

To find a percentage of a number, divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number.

Example: 15% of 200 = 15 ÷ 100 × 200 = 30.

How to calculate percentage change

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the absolute value of the original value, then multiply by 100.

A positive result means an increase, while a negative result means a decrease. A zero original value cannot be used as the denominator for percentage change.
