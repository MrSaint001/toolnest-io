What does the character counter measure?

Characters including spaces: Counts letters, numbers, punctuation, spaces and line breaks.

Characters without whitespace: Counts the text after whitespace characters are removed.

Letters: Counts Unicode letters, including letters with accents. Numbers, spaces and punctuation are excluded.

Words: Counts groups of text separated by whitespace.

Sentences: Estimates sentence endings using periods, question marks and exclamation marks.

Paragraphs: Counts non-empty sections separated by blank lines.

Counts update as you type. Results are calculated in your browser and your text is not sent to a server by this page.
