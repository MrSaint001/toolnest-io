Free Online Case Converter

Change the capitalization of your text instantly. Paste your text below and choose the case you need.

What is a case converter?

A case converter changes uppercase and lowercase letters without requiring you to retype the text. It is useful for editing headings, notes, documents, social posts and other writing.

Available text formats

Uppercase: Converts letters to capitals.

Lowercase: Converts letters to small letters.

Title case: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.

Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter at the start of a sentence.

Alternating case: Alternates lowercase and uppercase letters.

Is my text uploaded?

This tool processes your text in your browser. Its conversion code does not send the text to a ToolNest server.
