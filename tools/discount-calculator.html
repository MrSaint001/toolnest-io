How to calculate a discount

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100. Subtract the savings from the original price to get the sale price.

Example: A ₦50,000 item with a 20% discount saves ₦10,000. The final price is ₦40,000.

Who can use this calculator?

Shoppers can compare sale prices, while businesses can calculate promotional prices and savings. The result does not include additional taxes, delivery fees or other charges.
