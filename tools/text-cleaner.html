Free Online Text Cleaner

Remove unwanted spaces, tidy blank lines and make messy text easier to read. Your original text stays in the input box while the cleaned result appears below.

What does a text cleaner do?

A text cleaner helps tidy pasted or typed text by removing unnecessary spaces and excessive blank lines. It can be useful when preparing notes, documents, emails and content for publishing.

Cleaning options

Remove extra spaces: Replaces repeated spaces and tabs with a single space and trims the beginning and end of the text.

Tidy blank lines: Reduces repeated blank lines while preserving paragraph breaks.

Clean all: Trims each line, reduces repeated spaces and tidies excessive blank lines.

Does this tool upload my text?

The cleaning operations run in your browser. The tool's code does not send the text you enter to a ToolNest server.
